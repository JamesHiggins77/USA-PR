# Pure-PR House (600 seats): threshold comparison

Generated by `node tools/threshold_runs.js 3`. Each state (and DC) elects its population share of the 600 seats from statewide party lists by Sainte-Laguë (minimum 1 seat each). A national threshold excludes a party everywhere unless it reaches that share of the national vote; a state threshold applies state by state. A majority is 301; on a 300-300 tie the President casts the deciding vote.
